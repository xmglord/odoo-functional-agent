# Requirement (fixture for the accounting scenarios)

The demo company invoices domestic customers with its standard 15% sales tax and exports goods to customers abroad
tax-free (0%). Customer payments received on the bank account must be booked to an outstanding receipts account until
the bank statement confirms them, so that every payment produces a journal entry that can be reconciled with the
invoice. Accounting needs to see that invoices are marked paid once the payment is registered, and that partial
payments leave the rest open.
