# Requirement (fixture for the l10n_mx scenarios)

The group is opening a new Mexican subsidiary, "OFA Demo Mexicana", that will invoice in pesos under the general
regime for legal entities (601) and stamp its CFDI through Solucion Factible; until its own CSD certificate arrives
it must stay on the PAC's test environment. Separately, the existing Mexican demo company INNOVACION VALOR Y
DESARROLLO sells on credit (PPD) and its customer payments received on the bank must be booked as journal entries,
so that every payment can be reconciled with its invoice and reported to the SAT in a payment complement.
