# Odoo 20.0 knowledge index

One folder per area. Each area has its own `INDEX.md` that lists its topic entries. Mexican localization is a separate
area that the other areas link to from their `## Mexico notes` sections.

- [Sales and CRM](sales_crm/INDEX.md): leads and opportunities, pipelines and teams, quotations and sales orders,
  pricelists and discounts, quotation templates, and the settings that switch these features on. Start here for
  anything that happens before a customer invoice.
- [Purchase and Inventory](purchase_inventory/INDEX.md): vendors and purchase orders, purchase agreements, warehouses
  and locations, routes and operation types, receipts and deliveries, reordering rules, and product tracking (lots
  and serial numbers).
- [Accounting](accounting/INDEX.md): chart of accounts, journals, taxes and fiscal positions, customer invoices and
  vendor bills, payments and reconciliation, and payment terms. In 20.0, tax mapping lives on the tax, not on the
  fiscal position form.
- [Multi-company](multi_company/INDEX.md): holdings with several companies in one database: company hierarchy,
  which records are shared or company-specific, user access to companies, and inter-company flows.
- [Mexican localization](l10n_mx/INDEX.md): the `mx` chart of accounts, Mexican taxes and fiscal data on partners
  and companies, and electronic invoicing (CFDI). Entries cross-reference the other four areas.
